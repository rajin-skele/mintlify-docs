## Overview

The currently shipped Skelementor effects surface focuses on opacity utilities. The bundle includes every 5% step from `opacity-0` through `opacity-100`, which makes it easier to fine-tune overlays, disabled states, and layered interfaces.

## Current Effects Surface

| Family | Status | Notes |
| ------ | ------ | ----- |
| `opacity-*` | Shipped | Full 5% scale from `opacity-0` through `opacity-100` |

## Common Use Cases

- Surface layering and media dimming
- Disabled and loading states
- Subtle hierarchy shifts between primary and secondary content
- Overlay patterns that combine opacity with positioning and z-index
